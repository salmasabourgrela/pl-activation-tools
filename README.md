# Preserving Legacies Activation Tools

Interactive tools for Preserving Legacies Activation Sites.

## Inherent and Residual Impacts and Risks, Adaptation and Responses to Losses and Damages

A statement builder that helps custodians bring what they know about their heritage places and cultural practices together into clear, connected statements: from hazards, exposure and vulnerability, to impacts and inherent risk, and on to adaptation and loss and damage responses and the residual risk that remains.

- Tool: [`statement-builder/index.html`](statement-builder/index.html)
- Works in any modern browser. Once downloaded, it works without an internet connection.
- Work is saved in the user's own browser; *Save progress* keeps a file copy and *Export to Word* produces a report.

Developed by Salma Sabour for the Preserving Legacies Activation Sites programme, with contributions from the wider team, including Khansa Bouaziz, Tina Paterno, Sarah Forgesson, Victoria Herrmann and many others.
