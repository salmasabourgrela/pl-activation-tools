# Preserving Legacies Activation Tools

Interactive tools for Preserving Legacies Activation Sites.

## Inherent and Residual Impacts and Risks, Adaptation and Responses to Losses and Damages

A statement builder that helps custodians bring what they know about their heritage places and cultural practices together into clear, connected statements: from hazards, exposure and vulnerability, to impacts and inherent risk, and on to adaptation and loss and damage responses and the residual risk that remains.

- Version 6 (November 2026): [`statement-builder-6/index.html`](statement-builder-6/index.html). Version 4’s layout; explanations open only when you click a field or section title, and a Definitions panel gathers every definition in one searchable place. Definitions follow the Preserving Legacies glossary, in short form, with the full glossary wording one click away; IPCC and UNESCO definitions fill the gaps. Impact statements ask whether there are losses and damages (or whether they are not reported or not assessed) and which kinds, economic or non-economic.
- Version 5 (November 2026): [`statement-builder-5/index.html`](statement-builder-5/index.html). A step-by-step data-entry flow: about the place (entered once), the four statements, then review and export. Help and worked-example entries appear only when you click ? beside a field; examples open read-only.
- Version 4 (November 2026): [`statement-builder-4/index.html`](statement-builder-4/index.html). Hints and notes follow the IPCC AR6 glossary, with a collapsed key-terms panel; several hazards per statement; confidence uses the IPCC “medium”.
- Version 3 (November 2026): [`statement-builder-3/index.html`](statement-builder-3/index.html). Version 2’s layout with small fixes: plainer hints, text boxes that grow, a save status line, a clear-all dialog that offers a copy first, attribute and values carried into new statements, residual ratings worded as a comparison of judgements, and notes on the ratings.
- Version 2 (November 2026): [`statement-builder-2/index.html`](statement-builder-2/index.html), updated from reviewer comments. It adds the UNESCO definitions of integrity and authenticity, clearer hints and instructions, and removes the strategic pathway field from response statements.
- Version 1: [`statement-builder/index.html`](statement-builder/index.html)
- Works in any modern browser. Once downloaded, it works without an internet connection.
- Work is saved in the user's own browser; *Save progress* keeps a file copy and *Export to Word* produces a report.

Developed by Salma Sabour for the Preserving Legacies Activation Sites programme, with contributions from the wider team, including Khansa Bouaziz, Tina Paterno, Sarah Forgesson, Victoria Herrmann and many others.
