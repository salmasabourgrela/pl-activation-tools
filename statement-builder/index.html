Inherent and Residual Impacts and Risks, Adaptation and Responses to Losses and Damages

This tool helps you bring what you know about your heritage places and cultural practices together into clear, connected statements. It guides you from the hazards, exposure and vulnerability they face, to the impacts and risks that follow, and on to the adaptation and loss and damage responses that shape what remains, today and in the future.

How to use this tool

  • Explore a worked example. Choose one under Worked example and click Add example to see how a full set of four statements fits together. You can edit or remove the examples freely; changes stay on your device.
  • Start your own statements. Click + New statement and choose a type. A full set usually moves from impacts and inherent risk to responses and residual risk, first for today and then for the future.
  • Fill in the fields. The hints under each field show how to phrase it, so the parts join up into one connected statement. The draft builds as you type, with each element in its own colour. When it reads well, click Use as final statement and make it your own by adding local names, voices and context.
  • Rate each statement. Impact intensity × likelihood places an inherent risk statement on the matrix. Link each response statement to the inherent risk it acts on to see how far the responses reduce it.
  • Save your work. Your statements stay in this browser. Use Save progress to keep a copy as a file, and Export to Word for your report.
