Inherent and Residual Impacts and Risks, Adaptation and Responses to Losses and Damages

This tool helps you bring what you know about your heritage places and cultural practices together into clear, connected statements. It guides you from the hazards, exposure and vulnerability they face, to the impacts and risks that follow, and on to the adaptation and loss and damage responses that shape what remains, today and in the future.

How it works

Five steps, key terms and notes on the ratings
  1. Explore a worked example. Choose a worked example to see how four statements fit together. You can edit or delete them freely; changes apply only to your copy of the tool.
  2. Create your own statements. Click Start your own set and name the heritage place or practice. A full set pairs impacts and inherent risk with responses and residual risk, for both present and future conditions. Details such as the attribute and its values carry over between the statements in a set.
  3. Build and refine each statement. Work through Describe, Rate and Finalise. The hints under each field help the parts read as one connected statement, and the draft beside the fields updates as you type, with each element shown in its own colour. In Finalise, start from the draft and refine the wording with local names, voices and context.
  4. Rate the risk. Impact intensity × likelihood determines where an inherent risk statement appears on the matrix. Link each response statement to the inherent risk it addresses to compare your inherent and residual ratings.
  5. Save and export. Your work is saved in this browser as you type. Use Download a copy to keep a separate file, and Export to Word to create a document for your report. Use Clear all (under More) to remove existing statements and start fresh.
