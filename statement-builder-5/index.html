Inherent and Residual Impacts and Risks, Adaptation and Responses to Losses and Damages

Turn what you know about a heritage place or practice into four connected statements: its present and future climate risks, the responses to them, and the risk that remains.

How it works
  • Name the heritage place or practice, then give its attribute and values once.
  • Fill in the four statements, one step at a time. Click ? beside a field for a short explanation and a worked example.
  • The tool writes each statement from your entries. In Review and export you can edit the wording, then export to Word.
  • Your work is saved in this browser. Use Save a file to keep a copy you can open later or share.
About the ratings
    Key terms (IPCC definitions)

    Statements follow the Preserving Legacies Reporting Guidelines. Terms follow the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report glossary (2022); integrity and authenticity follow UNESCO (2026). Once downloaded, this file works without an internet connection. Version 5, November 2026.

    Developed by Salma Sabour for the Preserving Legacies Activation Sites programme, with contributions from the wider team, including Khansa Bouaziz, Tina Paterno, Sarah Forgesson, Victoria Herrmann and many others.
